Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

Welcome to ClipPulse Studio ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have in relation to it when you use our video formatting and publishing services.

1. Information We Collect

When you use ClipPulse Studio, we may collect the following information:

  • Account Authorization Data: If you connect your social media or third-party accounts (such as TikTok via OAuth 2.0), we receive authorization tokens (Access Token and Refresh Token) and basic profile identifiers (e.g., Creator Username / Open ID) necessary to interact with third-party APIs on your behalf.
  • User-Submitted Content: Video files, descriptions, tags, and publishing preferences (privacy status, comment permissions) submitted for processing and export.
  • Technical Logs: Minimal error logs and processing status required for reliable operation.

2. How We Use TikTok API Data

Our application interacts with the TikTok Content Posting API to facilitate content publication. Specifically:

  • We request only the scopes necessary for core functionality (video.publish, video.upload, and user.info.basic).
  • We use access tokens strictly to upload videos and apply metadata (such as titles, captions, privacy settings, duet/stitch permissions) explicitly chosen by the user.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your TikTok user data or access tokens with third parties.
  • We do not access private messages, followers, or personal financial details.

3. Data Storage & Security

All OAuth authorization tokens and user data are stored securely on private servers using encryption protocols. Temporary video files generated during editing or slicing are automatically purged from our servers once the processing job is completed or after a short retention period.

4. Revocation of Access & Data Deletion

You maintain full control over your connected accounts at all times:

  • Revoking Access via TikTok: You can disconnect ClipPulse Studio at any time by going to your TikTok App → Settings & Privacy → Security & permissions → Manage app permissions and removing ClipPulse Studio.
  • Deleting Data: You may request complete deletion of any stored tokens or logs by contacting us or issuing a disconnect command in our application. Upon request, all associated credentials will be permanently erased.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational or regulatory changes. Updated versions will be posted directly on this page with an updated revision date.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us at: support@clippulsestudio.internal.
